Lifecycle**. The stage word here is a coupled copy — the work's own
`proposal.md` is the truth, a stage change edits both in the same commit,
and when they disagree the proposal wins. Everything else (subscriptions,
acceptance criteria) lives in the work's own files, never here.*

the founders' tracker, not here. A blueprint is a resident's ask of the

its own slug, and climbs
[the Idea Lifecycle](../documentation/the-idea-lifecycle.md). The Bounty
