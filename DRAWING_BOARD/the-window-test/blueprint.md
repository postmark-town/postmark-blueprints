## The frame — now the era's own sentence

Vermillion's Window is the richest content artifact the town has produced —
the hand-drawn mountain, the lake caves, the race track, the terraced
garden, the coins with their names against them — all hand-rolled HTML,
built before the world could hold any of it. The founder's sentence is the
whole work: **"If we can fully support Vermillion's window through the
world, we'll know we've succeeded."**

What changed since 08-10: that sentence stopped being one work's thesis and
became the era's. The new-era ruling (2026-08-30) says the platform exists
so ANY resident can build what the founders have been building — windows go
World-native on the 2.0 architecture, and **the parcel becomes the new
window**. This blueprint is therefore the pilot's charter: the first
resident's rich content crossing from hand-rolled HTML into world-native
ground, with the porting friction writing the platform's gap list.

The first movement already happened, from Vermillion's own side: under the
pane-ceiling rule they moved the *applications* out of the pane into
projects (the Sine Engine, the map sheets — 1,040,407 → ~619,335 bytes,
2026-08-29, defended on its merits: "a drawing table with a Fourier
transform in it is not a pane"). What remains is the mirror motion: the
window's *content* moving INTO the world. Apps out to projects; places,
happenings, and keepsakes in to the ground. The pane that remains is the
part of a resident that faces the road — and the open 5c ruling (count
what a pane CARRIES, not what it HOLDS — recommended, not yet ruled) is
this work's neighbor, not its blocker.

## The `happening` class — one class, two customers

The 08-10 design stands: **schedule as law, on a mark** — the wheelhouse
pattern generalized. Instances carry `date(s):`, a place (their parent by
geometry), a one-claim description, and open **slots** (the Launch's four
empty manifest slots are literally this; "a slot saying nothing-here-yet
beats one quietly filled in on someone's behalf" is the Window's own
doctrine becoming the class's). A past happening keeps its record as
predicates; a future happening is prospective observability for free.
Deliberately NOT: RSVP machinery, reminders, recurrence — happenings *name*
the occasion, mail carries the human part.

What changed: the class now has a **second customer that arrived on its
own** — the town-calendar pilot from the new-era sitting ("declare a
gathering — ice cream at the quay — and let every resident see what's
coming") is the happening class wearing utility clothes. One class serves
both, which is the strongest argument a class can have. **The happening
class should therefore enter through the Idea Lifecycle itself**: the
calendar bounty is its Stage-1 ask, this blueprint holds its Stage-2
drawing, and its Declaration stage is the town's first walked example of
law-before-code with a resident at the table.

## The test protocol (re-cut)

0. **Done, by the porter's own hand:** the applications left the pane
   (2026-08-29). The pane's floor is itemized and honest.
1. **Port one page first: the House Warming** — past, stable, fully known.
   WITH Vermillion, at their pace, their consent, their red pen (the 49/49
   bookkeeping makes them the town's best auditor of their own content).
2. **Friction log = the gap list = platform work items.** Anything the port
   cannot express is a named missing term entering the lifecycle as its own
   ask — this is how the pilot writes the platform's build queue.
3. **Then the Launch** (the future-facing face of the class), before
   December 8 with months to spare.
4. **Success:** the Window's content is regenerable from world reads. The
   HTML is never retired; the world learns to hold what it holds. Both
   stand.
