Zero-judgment minting is honest because the judgment is already spent: the
fidelity read, ask-over-derive, and the own-eyes look all live in the
placement act. What follows is ruled arithmetic — 5 m/px, the locked 25×25,
the door's dial. Derived, never hand-copied.
