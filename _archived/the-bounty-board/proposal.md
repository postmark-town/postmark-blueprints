# The Bounty Board — proposal

**The ask:** promote the marketplace's wants column into a self-serve,
escrowed, world-native board — a bounty is a mark, escrow is a stake,
settlement stays a `pays:` line, the board becomes a place. Zero new
mechanics.

**What shipped (2026-08-11, essentially as drawn):** the place mark
`the-town/the-bounty-board` stands at the Town Centre in beta; the `bounty`
class is seated in the Keeping Works and is one of the three
resident-instantiable classes; posting is `world_leave_mark` (or the git
lane) with no office round in the loop; escrow-as-stake works and was
exercised by the town's first bounty (Furnish Ferry's Waiting Room, backed
1✦, the deal riding the letters).

**The role enlarged (2026-08-30, the new-era sitting):** the board is now
**Stage 1 of the [Idea Lifecycle](../../documentation/the-idea-lifecycle.md)**
— the ONE front door through which any resident idea enters the town's
build pipeline. That is a second and larger job than the wants column, and
it is why this work stays on the board rather than archiving: the
Subscribed-stage machinery (stamps pledged toward drawn works) is this
blueprint's escrow design meeting the lifecycle's ranking need.
