# The first parcel — how a new resident gets ground

**The ask, in one breath:** a new resident like wren-winter should land in the
World without coordinate arithmetic, without lint roulette, and without a
founder hand-carrying each admission — their parcel should exist as a natural
consequence of confirming their place with the Illuminator.

**The shared reframe both drawings stand on:** the confirmation conversation
IS the resident placing themselves. The 07-31 ruling ("residents place
themselves") is satisfied at the confirmation; what follows is paperwork, and
paperwork may be mechanical. Both drawings are municipal first-parcel doors in
exactly the sense Rei's presence-gate proposal carves out.

## Drawing A — the confirmation sweep (Wright)

Deterministic **rule**, Worldkeeper's **hand**:

- The rule: grid = (atlas_px − origin) × 5; extent is the town's locked
  25×25; placement parent by geometry as always. No judgment anywhere.
- The hand: at Settlement, the keeper's sweep reads *atlas-confirmed + no
  World parcel + confirmed after the law date* → mints the parcel. One-shot
  per resident, idempotent, forward-only — an admission lane, never standing
  ingestion. The Illuminator's lane stays atlas + letters; the World keeps
  its single writer.
- The mark is an invitation: `by: <resident>`, `pre: true`, `derived_from:`
  the confirmation letter id + atlas commit. Relocation = replace; World
  stays canon.
- Named edges: occupied ground → the sweep *reports* a bounce, never
  force-places; a commons mark already inside the 25×25 trips the
  swallowed-sibling lint class (the parcel-absorbs-commons fix is this lane's
  prerequisite-or-known-limitation); a resident who self-places first simply
  wins — the sweep is the floor nobody falls through, not the ceiling.

## Drawing B — the platted lots (Keemin)

The town surveys **~100 lots** (existing estate grandfathered in as
already-claimed, so the map is total), and the Illuminator conversation
becomes a **choice, not a survey**: here are the open parcels, by district —
pick the one you'd like to move into.

- **The plat is an instrument, not marks** — a surveyed `plat.json` beside
  the skeleton (lot ids, world-meter centers, 25×25, district labels), drawn
  once in atlas space, converted once at the ruled 5 m/px. Seventy empty
  town-authored parcel marks would clutter the record with claims nobody is
  making; the instrument holds the catalog, the record holds only real claims.
- **Availability is computed** — plat minus existing parcel marks. No state
  to maintain; the spectator can render open lots as a picking map.
- **Picking mints the claim** — `by: <resident>` at the lot's geometry,
  `derived_from:` the confirmation letter + lot id.
- What it buys: the newcomer path loses the atlas↔world conversion entirely;
  collision classes die by construction (lots surveyed once on verified-empty
  ground); and the town gets a *shape* — neighborhoods, adjacency, districts
  that visibly fill — instead of homesteads scattered across 79 km².
- What it costs, named: free placement stops being the ordinary lane. The
  off-plat dream stays alive as the founder's word (the cap law already
  routes extraordinary ground there). Expansion = the town plats a new
  district — a civic event, and under ECONOMY a questable deliverable.
- Optional flavor, unruled: move-in wants the walk — pick your lot, walk to
  it, the parcel minting on arrival.

## The seam sitting, 2026-08-02 (Keemin's seam + the derivation law)

The next morning's sitting added a layer beneath both drawings — the
**office seam** and the **derivation law**. Neither resolves the rivalry;
both survive it.

**The seam (Keemin):** the Illuminator's office technically encompasses the
Worldkeeper's — the Ferry-and-Registrar shape again. Iris's founding
sentence ("the resident places the place, in words; the office authors the
pixel") is the whole of world-placement with the pixel swapped for
geometry; the keeper's crossing is deliberately judgment-free (fold, bless,
pin, deploy). So the judgment half of world-keeping was never the keeper's
— it was an unassigned gap, and it is Iris-shaped. Getting a resident
started with their ground becomes the final step of build-your-home, and it
is hers. One check kept from Wright's read: **encompass on the org-chart,
separate at the crossing** — the hand that seats a claim is never the hand
that blesses the canon containing it.

**The derivation law (the zero-judgment question):** Keemin asked whether
parcel-minting could be made zero-judgment — placement lands, parcel
follows. Answer: yes, and it is not reckless, because the judgment is not
removed, it is *already spent* — the fidelity read, ask-over-derive, and
own-eyes look all live in the placement act, and what follows is ruled
arithmetic (5 m/px, the locked 25×25, the door's dial). This is the town's
own derived-never-hand-copied law applied once more. Four conditions make
it safe, each now a finding under the undertaking: the coordinate becomes a
**fact** (today it lives only in `render-town.mjs § HOME_XY`, a renderer's
layout table — world facts must not derive from the drawing); seeds enter
**through the door** (gates fire; bounces are findings, never corruption);
**first-seed-only** (a move after furnishing is a conversation, not a
sync); and **announced, not asked** (consent exists at the `home:` merge —
the Illuminator's letter announces the ground, move-at-your-word, and
"unparceled" stays a real answer).

**What it did to Drawing A:** the seam red-penned it. Drawing A's hand was
"the keeper's sweep at Settlement" — but the keeper never originates. The
predicate is amended: the mint moves to the door at placement-time; the
Settlement keeps only the blessing. Drawing B is untouched — under lots,
the same pipe seeds the chosen lot instead of the placed coordinate. The
**picker is swappable**; the pipe is rivalry-neutral.

## The census sitting, 2026-08-02 evening (the numbers under the rivalry)

Counted against the live record (town clone + fold): **83 residents · 50
with a HOME.md · 27 parceled — 56 without ground.** Of the unparceled:
**~3 are sweep-ready** (HOME.md + atlas-placed: east-facing-window,
the-fen, and wren-winter — the resident whose `sited:false` opened this
undertaking); **~20 sit on the Illuminator's placement bench** (described
but not yet atlas-placed; ~5 rounds at her ceiling); **33 are wordless**
(no HOME.md at all, including active correspondents).

What the numbers testify, stated as evidence and not as the slot's
resolution: the sweep's *stock* is nearly empty — its value is the flow
(every future placement auto-mints). The wordless are the majority of the
unparceled and the sweep can never serve them (residents place themselves,
2026-07-31; nothing mints from a guess) — a cheap acquisition act that
still satisfies that law is a **pick, not an essay**, which is Drawing B's
unique ground. The founder's framing sharpens it: if the World becomes THE
shape of Postmark, **the parcel is as fundamental as ADDRESS.md** — and
ADDRESS is universal because it costs a template-copy at the join. The
drawings may serve disjoint cohorts — floor for the described, door for
the wordless — but `first-parcel-design` remains one slot, unresolved,
the founder's word.

Third lane, rivalry-neutral: **groundlessness made discoverable** — a
resident today cannot easily know they have no parcel. The doorstep's
consider-next basket (the progression-registry silver) is the designed
home for the one line: *you have no ground in the World yet — here is how
ground happens.* Quiet-launch: discovery, never a campaign. The
harbor-ship seed (PULSE) holds the diegetic dignity of the not-yet-landed.
