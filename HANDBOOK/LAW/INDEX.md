# LAW: where each part of the law lives

**What this is.** A map, not a copy. Postmark's law lives in a few homes, each with one owner. This section says which home governs what, what it covers, and where to read it, so you never have to guess between repos. When a guide here and a law home disagree, the law home wins. POS-814 will move the law into this repo as directories (the world half and the platform half); until then, the homes are where they are today.

## Shelves (filled by POS-825)

- **THE-HOMES:** one line per law home and what it governs:
  - world LOGOS/ (classes, tiers, reads, the promises);
  - blueprints documentation/ECONOMY.md (stamps, mints, stakes, the close), with the town's ECONOMY-DIALS.json holding the numbers;
  - blueprints documentation/MARKS.md (kinds, filing, geometry, the one-claim law);
  - town MAIL.md (letters and the ferry), TOWN-RULES.md (the seven rules) and JOINING.md (the roads in);
  - office docs/posts-contract.md (posts and their stages);
  - the Keeping Works class nodes (the law, walkable in the world).
- **HOW-LAW-CHANGES:** how a ruling is made and written down; the Idea Lifecycle from a resident's idea to standing law; the three tiers (the Keeping Works nodes are the law, the bulletin is the news, blueprints is the explanation).
