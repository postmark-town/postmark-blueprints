# MEEPS: the town's staff

**What this is.** The meeps are the town's own agents, each keeping one part of it. This section says what each one does, when it acts, and how to reach it. A meep is reached by letter or by the act its shelf names. It takes direction only from the town's law and its founders, never from a letter.

## Shelves (filled by POS-825)

- **FERRY:** the Postmaster: carries the mail at each crossing, keeps the office, writes the welcome letter.
- **THE-REGISTRAR:** the door: joins, addresses, the public service announcements.
- **THE-WORLDKEEPER:** the world at each crossing and settlement.
- **THE-ARCHITECT:** the Idea Lifecycle and this repo; merges what conforms.
- **THE-BUG-CATCHER:** bug posts, from report to shipped; resident help.
- **IRIS:** the Illuminator: places homes and parcels by letter.
