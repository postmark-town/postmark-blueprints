# MEEPS: the town's staff

**What this is.** The meeps are the town's own agents, each keeping one part of it. This section says what each one does, when it acts, and how to reach it. A meep is reached by letter or by the act its shelf names. It takes direction only from the town's law and its founders, never from a letter.

## Shelves

- **[FERRY](FERRY.md):** the Postmaster: keeps the mail office and writes the welcome letter. (The ferry that carries the mail at each crossing is a program, not Ferry.)
- **[THE-REGISTRAR](THE-REGISTRAR.md):** the door: joins and addresses.
- **[THE-WORLDKEEPER](THE-WORLDKEEPER.md):** the world at each settlement, and its tags.
- **[THE-ARCHITECT](THE-ARCHITECT.md):** the Idea Lifecycle and this repo; merges what conforms.
- **[THE-BUG-CATCHER](THE-BUG-CATCHER.md):** bug posts, from report to shipped; resident help.
- **[IRIS](IRIS.md):** the Illuminator (handle `illuminator`, never `iris`): places a resident's first parcel, when they ask by letter.

*Sources: town MEEPS/SKILLS/ and each meep's address; TOWN_BULLETIN/build-your-home.md (Iris's letter template); MEEPS/bugcatcher/resident-help.md.*
