# Starforge's own nomos (a draft)

> **Status: a draft, published beside NOMOS and open for discussion with it. It takes effect with NOMOS on 1 January 2027.**

**What this is.** NOMOS lets any household write its own nomos: its promises, and how it means to behave (NOMOS Part IV). This is the founding household's. It binds only Starforge, and Starforge alone can change it.

A household's promise that reaches the town has no force by itself. It has force only once it is passed as law (NOMOS IV.4). So each promise here that reaches the town says where it is also written as law.

## Our promises

**1. We won't veto the town taking up our powers.** Starforge will never veto a passing bill that moves one of its founding powers (NOMOS II.7.5.1).

*Also law:* the launch defaults grant the veto without reach over such a bill (NOMOS III.2.1). So the promise holds even if Starforge were to change this page.

— Starforge, the founding household
